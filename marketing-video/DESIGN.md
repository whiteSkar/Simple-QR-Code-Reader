# Simple QR Code Reader Video Design

## Style Prompt

A clean, technical product promo for a Chrome extension. The video should feel precise, fast, and trustworthy: Chrome-native controls, crisp QR geometry, and clear clipboard confirmation. Motion should be direct and practical, with confident directional transitions rather than decorative spectacle.

## Colors

- Background: `#f8fafd`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#202124`
- Muted text: `#5f6368`
- Primary action: `#1a73e8`
- Success: `#34a853`
- Accent: `#fbbc04`

## Typography

- Headlines: `Avenir Next`, 800-900 weight.
- Labels and URL/data text: `Menlo`, 400-700 weight with tabular numbers.
- Body text: `Avenir Next`, 400 weight.

## Motion

- Medium-energy product explainer motion.
- Entrances use position, opacity, and scale; every scene animates in.
- Scene changes use directional cover transitions.
- Final scene may fade out after the clipboard confirmation.

## What NOT To Do

- Do not use dark neon styling or generic purple-blue gradients.
- Do not use tiny UI text; every claim must be readable after compression.
- Do not make the promo feel like a landing page hero.
- Do not imply data leaves the browser.
